User Interface
==============

Button Functionality
--------------------

Home/Cancel
~~~~~~~~~~~
* Displays the home screen (when on an information screen or in menu)
* Cancels the current selection without saving (when editing a setting)

Alarm
~~~~~
* Displays the alarm screen with all currently active alarms

Run/Reset
~~~~~~~~~
* Press to cycle run/pause
* Press and hold to reset the current dose cycle

Menu Up/Down
~~~~~~~~~~~~
* Enters the menu (when on the home screen)
* Cycles through the menu selections (when in menu)
* Increments or decrements the selection (when editing a setting)

Screen Left/Right
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
* Cycles through the various information screens (when on the home screen)
* Right arrow enters submenus (when in menu)
* Selects the character or digit (when editing certain settings)

Select/Save
~~~~~~~~~~~
* Enters the menu (when on the home screen)
* Selects a setting to edit or submenu to enter (when in a menu)
* Saves the current setting (when editing a setting)

Information Screens
-------------------

From the home screen, pressing the left and right buttons cycles through the information screens.

Dose Info
~~~~~~~~~
Describes the current state of the FLORATek control with some additional details.

Solution Levels
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
When consumption tracking is enabled, this screen illustrates the current approximate solution levels inside of your containers.

Pump Assignments
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Provides a quick reference for your current pump configuration.

About
~~~~~
Provides information about your FLORATek 4 control along with diagnostic information.

2-hour EC Graph
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
A quick-reference graph of the measured EC for the past 2 hours. Each pixel along the x-axis represents approximately one minute. The dotted line represents your current EC setpoint.

2-hour pH Graph
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
A quick-reference graph of the measured pH for the past 2 hours. Each pixel along the x-axis represents approximately one minute. The dotted line represents your current pH setpoint, with a y-axis range of +/-2pH.

24-hour EC Graph
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
A quick-reference graph of the measured EC for the past 24 hours. Each pixel along the x-axis represents approximately 12 minutes. The dotted line represents your current EC setpoint.

24-hour pH Graph
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
A quick reference graph of the measured pH for the past 24 hours. Each pixel along the x-axis represents approximately 12 minutes. The dotted line represents your current pH setpoint, with a y-axis range of +/-2pH.

Menus
-----

From the home screen, pressing up, down, or select will enter the main menu. From the main menu, you can navigate to the various submenus to configure your settings and access functions.

.. note::

   Depending on your current software version and configuration, you may see more or fewer options than are described here.

Main Menu
~~~~~~~~~

Dosing Setup
  Frequently-adjusted settings pertaining to your current nutrient schedule and grow cycle.

System Setup
  Configuration settings that are typically adjusted infrequently.

Probes and Sensors
  Information and functions related to probe maintenance.

Prime Pumps
  Functions for priming all connected dosing pumps.

Connections
  Information and settings pertaining to connecting your control to the internet or to other remote pumps and sensors.

Data Logging
  Settings and functions related to the on-board data logging feature.
